# Code of conduct

We want Atlas to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and the community Discord is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Not harass, discriminate, or publish other people's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block contributors, who do not follow these rules. Report problems to **support@modhyt.org**; reports are handled privately.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
